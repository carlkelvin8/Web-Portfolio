FAQ

Questions
founders ask.

How much does an MVP cost?

Fixed prices start at $4,900 for an MVP build, $7,900 for a mobile app with an API, and $1,900 for an admin dashboard. Your exact price depends on features and platforms. On the free 15-min call I’ll give you a ballpark, and after scoping you get one fixed quote, so you know the full cost before any work starts.

How long does it take?

Typical timelines: an MVP build takes 6-8 weeks, a mobile app with an API 8-12 weeks, and an admin dashboard 2-4 weeks. You get a timeline for your project as part of the scope.

Who owns the code?

You do, 100%. At launch I hand over the source code, deployments, and accounts, so you’re never locked in to me.

What happens after launch?

Every project includes one month of free post-launch support for bug fixes and small adjustments. If you want to keep building after that, we can agree on the next phase.

I only have an idea. Is that enough?

Yes. Scoping is part of the work: we turn the idea into features, screens, and a plan. You don’t need designs or technical specs to start.

Do you work with founders outside the Philippines?

Yes. I work remotely, and my work is live in the Philippines and Norway. We agree on overlapping hours for calls and keep everything else async.

What tech do you use?

Mostly Next.js and React for web, React Native for iOS and Android, and Laravel or Node.js with PostgreSQL or Supabase on the backend. I pick what fits your product, not what’s trendy.
